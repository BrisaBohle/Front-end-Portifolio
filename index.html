About

I'm an architect with a Master's in Sustainability. But the real part of who I am: I'm curious and I notice patterns.

I hold an M.Sc. in Sustainable Architecture from NTNU and a degree in Architecture and Urbanism from Brazil, giving me an international perspective that combines design sensibility with analytical and technical thinking.

My work focuses on the intersection between the built environment, technology, and user needs. I am especially drawn to problems where architectural domain knowledge, structured tool evaluation, and fast independent learning are directly useful.

I have contributed to 55+ architectural projects in Norway and spent four years in an internal innovation initiative at Mestergruppen Arkitekter, where I worked across BIM production, digital tool evaluation, workflow automation, parametric design prototyping, and AI-assisted workflow exploration.

Current focus: roles where I can apply architecture expertise to evaluate, prototype, and improve digital tools for the AEC sector. I'm learning Cowork and deepening my skills in Python and AI-assisted workflows.
